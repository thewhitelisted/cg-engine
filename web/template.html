How this was built

Each pick is chosen to maximise your chance of winning the title in this league's exact format: 19 regular-season weeks ranked by categories won, then an 8-team bracket over Mar 8 to Mar 28. For every candidate the tool simulates the rest of the draft and the season hundreds of times.

Player forecasts combine ESPN's projections, a model of each player's last three seasons, and the draft market. How much the market knows changes from year to year: in 2021-22 and 2023-24 it was clearly right where it disagreed with projections, in 2025-26 it was not. So the market's weight is not a fixed number. Each simulated season draws its own weight from the range seen across past seasons, which makes the tool favour players where the market and the projections agree and demand more before reaching on a player far ahead of his ADP.

One more correction matters a lot in this league: ESPN's ADP follows ESPN's points-league ranking almost exactly, while your league scores 9 categories. The tool separates that format difference from the market's real information and only lets the information move its forecasts. That is why points-league favourites can sit below their ADP here and category specialists above it.

These forecasts were chosen by a pre-registered test that scores the whole predicted distribution of every player's season on seasons held out from fitting. Games played are recalibrated, players on last season's weak teams are discounted in the fantasy playoffs, and weekly uncertainty is calibrated against real weeks.

Honest limits: three clean past seasons cannot say whether 2026-27 will be a year where the market is right; the tool hedges rather than guesses. Any single season can swing on injuries to a few players near your pick. Every decision and test is logged in DECISIONS.md.
