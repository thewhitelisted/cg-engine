How these numbers are made

The regular season is played out on the real remaining schedule, each matchup with that week's NBA games. Seeding follows the category record (H2H each category). Each playoff round is a single week; the semifinal losers meet for third. Prizes: $150 / $90 / $60.

Playoff-week games include late-season rest, which is heavier on teams that were weak the season before. These are model estimates, not guarantees.
