How these numbers are made

Category and head-to-head chances use a typical regular-season week. Title, playoff and prize odds play out the league's real 19-week schedule and the 8-team bracket, paying $150 / $90 / $60. Playoff-week strength counts each roster's games and late-season rest in the March 8–28 weeks.

These are model estimates built on the projections named at the top of the page. They are a guide, not a guarantee: projections miss, and early in the season the numbers still lean mostly on preseason projections.
